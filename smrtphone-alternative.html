    Before you switch

    smrtPhone alternative — questions investors ask

    Is REsimpli a replacement for smrtPhone?
    It replaces more than smrtPhone. smrtPhone is a phone system and dialer you connect to a separate CRM. REsimpli includes the single-line and triple-line dialer and a full real estate investor CRM, with standard calling minutes and SMS included based on your plan — so it replaces the phone tool, the separate per-seat dialer subscription, and the standalone CRM in one login.
    How is the pricing different?
    smrtPhone bills in three parts: a required core plan (Standard $75/mo · Pro $125/mo), a per-seat smrtDialer subscription (single-line $50/mo · multi-line $90/mo per seat), and metered usage for minutes and texts. REsimpli is one predictable plan price per tier — Basic $199, Pro $499, Enterprise $1,999 per month, billed annually (or $299 / $599 / $2,499 monthly) — with the dialers built in and a standard calling and SMS allocation included by plan (Basic 1,000/mo, Pro 5,000/mo, Enterprise unlimited under fair use). Additional standard usage is pay-as-you-go, and AI calling and AI SMS are billed separately.
    How much calling and texting is included?
    Standard calling minutes and SMS are included based on your plan: 1,000/mo on Basic, 5,000/mo on Pro, and unlimited under a fair-use policy on Enterprise. Additional standard usage beyond your allocation is available on a pay-as-you-go basis. AI calling and AI SMS are separately metered at a per-use rate. Either way, there's no separate per-seat dialer subscription.
    I use smrtPhone with a separate CRM. What happens to that?
    You can retire it. Because REsimpli has the CRM built in — pipeline, lead management, automation, dispo, and reporting — most investors consolidate their phone and dialer stack and their standalone CRM into REsimpli and cancel the extra subscriptions.
    Can I keep my current phone numbers?
    In all cases, numbers can be ported in, and our onboarding team walks you through the process so you do not lose your marketing numbers.
    Will switching disrupt my acquisitions team?
    Start a free trial and run REsimpli alongside your current setup before you move. Onboarding is free and unlimited, and support helps you import leads and get the team dialing quickly.
    What about smrtPhone's AI agents?
    smrtPhone offers smrtAgents on a per-use basis that work through your connected CRM. REsimpli's AI Engine (Gen 2) is built directly into the all-in-one CRM — it answers calls, qualifies sellers, follows up across weeks, and updates your pipeline, with full context because everything lives in one system.

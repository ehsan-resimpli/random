Before you switch

smrtPhone alternative — questions investors ask

Is REsimpli a replacement for smrtPhone?
It replaces more than smrtPhone. smrtPhone is a phone system and dialer you connect to a separate CRM. REsimpli includes the single-line and triple-line dialer and a full real estate investor CRM in one login — and on Enterprise you also get unlimited standard calling and SMS under fair use, so it replaces the phone tool, the per-seat dialer, and the standalone CRM.
How is the pricing different?
smrtPhone bills in three parts: a required core plan (Standard $75 / Pro $125 monthly), a per-seat smrtDialer subscription (single-line $50 / multi-line $90 per seat monthly), and metered usage for minutes and texts. REsimpli is one platform price per tier — Basic $199/mo billed yearly ($299 monthly), Pro $499/mo billed yearly ($599 monthly), and Enterprise $1,999/mo billed yearly ($2,499 monthly) — with the single- and triple-line dialer included on every plan and no per-seat dialer fees.
Is calling and texting really unlimited?
It depends on the plan. Basic includes 1,000 standard calling minutes and SMS per month, and Pro includes 5,000. Enterprise includes unlimited standard calling minutes and SMS under a fair-use policy — no per-minute or per-message meter for standard usage. On Basic and Pro, usage beyond the monthly allocation is pay-as-you-go. AI calling and AI SMS are always metered at a per-use rate on every plan. There are no per-seat dialer fees on any plan.
I use smrtPhone with a separate CRM. What happens to that?
You can retire it. Because REsimpli has the CRM built in — pipeline, lead management, automation, dispo, and reporting — most investors consolidate their phone and dialer stack and their standalone CRM into REsimpli and cancel the extra subscriptions.

What about smrtPhone's AI agents?
smrtPhone offers smrtAgents on a per-use basis that work through your connected CRM. REsimpli's AI Engine (Gen 2) is built directly into the all-in-one CRM — it answers calls, qualifies sellers, follows up across weeks, and updates your pipeline, with full context because everything lives in one system.
